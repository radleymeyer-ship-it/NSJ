Built on steel and straight answers

NSJ Builders and Steelwork is a registered building and steel fabrication company. We take the job from the drawing board to handover, and we stay on site until it's done properly.

Steel is our core trade. That means frames, gates, burglar bars and roof structures are made by people who know how much load a weld has to carry, not just how it looks.
